# Open-Source White-Label Business Catalog

**20,053 business ideas** in 938 categories: 11,969 open-source projects (11,215 distinct repos) you can self-host, rebrand and sell, 5,017 online store concepts and 3,067 white-label / reseller business models. Every repo was confirmed on GitHub on 2026-11-11; stars, license and archive status are as of then.

**Everything in one file:** [`Open-Source-White-Label-Business-Catalog.pdf`](Open-Source-White-Label-Business-Catalog.pdf) · spreadsheets: [`catalog.csv`](catalog.csv), [`stores.csv`](stores.csv), [`resell.csv`](resell.csv)

**License key** (what it means for white-labeling): 🟢 permissive (MIT/Apache/BSD), rebrand and sell freely, keep notices · 🟡 weak copyleft (MPL/LGPL), share changes to *their* files · 🟠 GPL, fine for hosted SaaS, distributed apps must ship source · 🔴 AGPL, hosted users must be offered your modified source · ⚪ other: NOASSERTION means GitHub couldn't auto-detect it (often dual/custom/source-available), NONE means no license file, read it first.

**Fit** (1-5): how directly the project can become a business. 5 = host or sell almost as-is · 4 = strong product, needs packaging · 3 = tool/component, build around it · 2 = library/framework, sell services or hosting around it. 📦 = archived repo (fork it). Not legal advice; trademarks and logos are never licensed, so always rebrand.

## How to use this

1. Pick a category, then filter by **Fit** (4-5 for products you can host almost as-is) and license (🟢/🟡 for the most freedom; 🟠/🔴 are fine for hosted services if you publish your changes).
2. Every project lists what it replaces, who buys it, 2-3 business models and 3 niche angles. The generic version usually exists already; the money is in the niche.
3. Rebrand (name, logo, domain, colours), add onboarding, billing and support. That is the product.
4. Open the CSVs in a spreadsheet to sort by fit, stars, license or effort and build a shortlist.
5. Reseller supply sources are starting points: check each provider's current partner terms, and expect licensing in insurance, lending, travel and health.
