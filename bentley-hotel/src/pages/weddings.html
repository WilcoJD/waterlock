From the ceremony to the morning after

How your day could run at The Bentley

Every wedding is different, so think of this as one way a full day here can unfold. If you’d rather hold just the meal, just the evening, or a small gathering in the conservatory, we’ll shape the day around that instead.

  1. The ceremony

    If you’d like to marry at the hotel

    Ask us about ceremonies when you enquire. We’ll talk you through where in the house a ceremony could take place, how the room would be set out and what needs to be arranged in advance. If you’d like somewhere to get ready beforehand, mention that too.

  2. Drinks

    A welcome in the conservatory

    After the ceremony, or as your guests arrive from a ceremony held elsewhere, gather for a drink in the conservatory while the photographs are taken.

  3. The meal

    Your wedding breakfast in the function suite

    Sit down together in the air-conditioned function suite, which seats up to 100 for a wedding breakfast. Your personalised menus and table plan, and fresh floral arrangements on every guest table, are part of our package.

  4. The evening

    A reception for up to 120, until 1am

    Evening guests join you for the reception, with room for up to 120 and a late licence until 1am. Cut your cake using our cake stand and knife, then dance until the end of the night.

  5. Overnight

    Your room at the hotel

    An overnight stay for the couple is included in our package, so when the last guests head home you’re already home for the night. Family and friends can stay too, in our 18 en-suite bedrooms.

  6. Next morning

    Breakfast with the people who stayed

    Our full Scottish breakfast is cooked to order, so there’s time to catch up with the guests who stayed over before everyone heads off. We’ll confirm what your overnight stay includes when we plan your wedding, and for guests booking their own rooms the booking confirmation shows whether breakfast is included.

Small weddings

Small weddings and intimate days

We’re pleased to host wedding parties of any size, from just 4 guests upwards, and a small wedding gets the same care as a large one.

For an intimate day, our conservatory is ideal: a bright glass room on the side of the house where a few close family and friends can sit down together for a wedding meal. The function suite is there if your numbers grow, or if you’d like a bigger evening celebration after a small ceremony and meal.

Ask us which parts of our wedding package suit a smaller day, and tell us if anyone is travelling to be with you so we can talk through rooms for them.

Planning with us

Five steps from first enquiry to the big day

Most couples arrive with a date, a rough number and a few firm ideas. That’s plenty to start with, and we’ll help you settle the rest.

It usually helps to see the rooms first and then settle the menu, the table plan and the timings over the following weeks or months. Whatever stage you’re at, a call or an enquiry is the place to start.

{{icon:info}}

Reception only, or evening only? The same steps apply. Just tell us which parts of the day you’d like to hold with us.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Send us your date, or a few dates you’re considering, and rough numbers for the day and the evening. We’ll come back to you with availability and how to go about holding your date.

  2. 2

    Come and see the function suite and the conservatory, walk through how your day could run, and ask us anything you like.

  3. 3

Wedding questions

Can we have our ceremony and reception at The Bentley?

Yes. The Bentley is a venue for both the ceremony and the reception. Tell us what kind of ceremony you have in mind when you enquire, and we’ll talk you through where it could take place and what needs to be arranged.

How many guests can you host?

We can host wedding parties of 4 to 100 guests, and evening receptions of up to 120. For smaller weddings, our conservatory is ideal; for larger ones, the air-conditioned function suite is exclusively yours.

Can we hold just the wedding meal, or just the evening reception, with you?

Yes. Some couples marry elsewhere and come to us for the meal, others hold a small ceremony and meal and then invite more guests for the evening. Tell us which parts of the day you’d like to hold here and we’ll plan around that.

Can our guests stay at the hotel?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite bedrooms, from singles to family rooms sleeping up to 3. Ask us about reserving rooms for your wedding guests when you enquire, and we’ll explain how they can confirm their bookings.

How late can the celebrations go on?

Our wedding package includes a late licence until 1am.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Our kitchen will talk through allergies, intolerances and dietary requirements for you and your guests when we plan your menu. Tell us too if children will be eating with you, and let us know about any requirements as early as you can.

Can we come and see the venue before we decide?

Of course. Call us on {{PHONE}} or send an enquiry and we’ll arrange a time to show you the function suite and the conservatory and talk through your plans.

Can we bring our own cake, band or photographer?

Yes. Our cake stand and knife are part of the package, ready for the cake you bring. Tell us who you’re working with for music, photography and anything else, and we’ll make sure they know where to go and what time to arrive on the day.
