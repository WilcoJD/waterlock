The restaurant

Comfortable, spacious and welcoming

Our kitchen cooks Scottish and British dishes, served to hotel guests and visitors alike, in the restaurant or the conservatory beside it.

The restaurant is in the original 1874 house. The conservatory was purpose-built onto the side of it, so you can move between the two: a table in the house for a quiet dinner, or a seat in the glass room on a bright day.

Whether you’re staying with us, meeting friends or gathering the family for a special occasion, you’ll find a warm welcome and food from our own kitchen.

Dietary needs & allergies

Tell us, and we’ll talk it through

If you have an allergy, intolerance or dietary requirement, we’d much rather know in advance. Our kitchen will talk you through the dishes and the ingredients we use, and tell you plainly what we can and can’t do.

{{icon:phone}}

When you book

Mention any allergies or requirements when you reserve your table, so the kitchen knows before you arrive.

{{icon:utensils}}

When you order

Remind your server when you order. They’ll check with the kitchen and go through the dishes and their ingredients with you.

{{icon:users}}

For a party or function

Send us a list of your guests’ requirements in advance. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or anything else: the more notice we have, the more we can offer.

Everything is prepared in the same kitchen, so if an allergy is severe please tell us, and we’ll discuss how we handle and prepare food before you decide what to order.

Food hygiene

Inspected and passed

Like other food businesses in Scotland, our kitchen is inspected by the local council under the Food Hygiene Information Scheme, which Food Standards Scotland runs with local authorities. Businesses that are inspected receive either a Pass or an Improvement Required result.

Bentley Hotel’s result is a Pass. The inspection was carried out by North Lanarkshire Council in December 2025, and you can check the result for yourself on the Food Standards Agency’s ratings site.

Restaurant questions

Do I need to book a table?

We’d recommend it, especially for dinner, weekends and groups. Opening times vary, so a quick call on {{PHONE}} saves a wasted journey and lets us have a table ready for you.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Families are a big part of what we do, from a meal out together to a christening lunch. Tell us how many children are coming and their ages when you book, and we’ll make sure you have a comfortable table.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary requirements?

Please tell us when you book and again when you order. We’ll explain what goes into each dish, and be honest about what we can and can’t do. For a party, send us your guests’ requirements in advance.

Can we hire the conservatory or a private room for a party?

Yes. The conservatory suits smaller parties that would like a space of their own, and for larger gatherings our function suite seats up to 100 for a meal. Send us the date and numbers and we’ll talk through the options and menus. There’s more on our functions page.

Planning a meal or a celebration?

Call the hotel to book a table, or send us the details of your occasion.
