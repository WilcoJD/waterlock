Travel

How to get here

{{icon:train}}

By train

Motherwell station is about a 5-minute walk away. It’s on the West Coast Main Line, with frequent ScotRail trains to Glasgow Central (the fastest take under 20 minutes) and services to Edinburgh. Direct trains also run to London Euston, Manchester and the north of England, and the Caledonian Sleeper stops here too.

{{icon:road}}

By car

Motherwell sits right beside the M74. Leave at junction 6 (A723), signed for Hamilton, Motherwell and Wishaw. The M8 between Glasgow and Edinburgh is a few miles to the north. Glasgow is about 12 miles away and Edinburgh about 30. Parking at the hotel is free.

{{icon:plane}}

By air

Glasgow Airport is roughly 20 miles away (around 30 minutes by car) and Edinburgh Airport roughly 30 miles (around 45 minutes), traffic permitting. Glasgow Prestwick is about 40 miles away.

Journey times are approximate and vary with the time of day. Check ScotRail or National Rail for current train times.

Recognising the house

We’re the sandstone villa with the tall square tower and the glass conservatory on the left, set back behind hedges on High Road, next door to the North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre. The house dates from 1874, so it looks nothing like a modern hotel, which is rather the point.

On the doorstep

Things to do nearby

From country parks and a theme park to football at Fir Park, there’s plenty within a couple of miles. And Glasgow’s big venues are a short train ride away.

{{icon:castle}}

Next door

North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre

A free attraction telling the area’s story from Roman times, with a viewing tower over the Clyde Valley and the North Lanarkshire archives.

{{icon:tree}}

About a mile

Strathclyde Country Park

A large country park beside the Clyde, with a loch, watersports, woodland walks and the remains of a Roman bathhouse. It hosted the 2014 Commonwealth Games triathlon.

{{icon:ticket}}

About 2 miles

M&D’s Theme Park & Amazonia

“Scotland’s Theme Park”, on the edge of Strathclyde Country Park, with rollercoasters and fairground rides, plus Amazonia, an indoor tropical house.

{{icon:football}}

About a mile

Fir Park

Home of Motherwell FC since 1895. Visiting for a match? Book early to secure your room.

{{icon:tree}}

About a mile

Dalzell Estate & Baron’s Haugh

Woodland walks around Dalzell House, with Japanese gardens and an arboretum, next to the RSPB’s Baron’s Haugh wetland reserve, where kingfishers and otters can be spotted.

{{icon:star}}

A few miles

Ravenscraig & Chatelherault

The Ravenscraig Regional Sports Facility, and Chatelherault Country Park with William Adam’s 18th-century hunting lodge, are both a short drive away.

Visiting for…

Whatever brings you to Motherwell

Many of our guests come for a reason: a match, a concert, a meeting or a day out with the family. Here’s how The Bentley fits in with each.

{{icon:football}}

A match at Fir Park

Fir Park is about a mile from the hotel: close enough to walk to the ground and back. If you’re coming for a match, book your room as soon as the fixture is confirmed. If you’d like a meal before kick-off or a drink afterwards, call ahead and we’ll check the restaurant and bar times for you.

A concert or match in Glasgow

The OVO Hydro, Hampden Park and Celtic Park are all within about 15 miles, and direct trains run from Motherwell to Glasgow Central, the fastest in under 20 minutes. Leave the car in our free car park and travel in by train. Check the time of the last train back before you set off, and ask us about a taxi if you’ll be later than that.

A day in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is about 30 miles away. ScotRail runs trains from Motherwell to Edinburgh, though fewer than to Glasgow, so check the timetable the night before. By car, the M8 is a few miles north of the hotel. Motherwell sits between the two cities, so a day in each can be done from one base.

The town

Motherwell town centre

The Bentley is at the west end of Motherwell, on High Road, with the town centre a short way up the road.

In the town you’ll find the Brandon Shopping Centre and Motherwell Concert Hall & Theatre, along with the shops, cafés and services you’d expect of a town of Motherwell’s size. The North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre, next door to the hotel, is the place to start if you’d like to understand how the steel town grew up around these streets.

Our own corner of the town is quieter: a street of Victorian sandstone villas in the Hamilton Road Conservation Area, with the bowling club beside us and the station a 5-minute walk away. If you’re curious about the house itself, there’s more on our history page.

Getting here: your questions

What should I put in my sat nav?

Our postcode, ML1 3HU, or the full address: The Bentley Hotel, 19 High Road, Motherwell. The nearest motorway exit is junction 6 of the M74.

Is there parking, and does it cost anything?

Yes, and no. Our car park is on site and free for hotel guests, and for people eating in the restaurant or attending a function. If you’re bringing a minibus or anything larger than a car, call us first so we can advise.

How far is the station, and where can I get to from it?

Motherwell station is about 420 metres away, around a 5-minute walk. It’s on the West Coast Main Line, with frequent trains to Glasgow Central (the fastest in under 20 minutes), services to Edinburgh, and direct trains to London Euston and Manchester. The Caledonian Sleeper stops here too. Check National Rail for times.

Which airport is closest?

Glasgow Airport, about 20 miles away or around 30 minutes by car. Edinburgh Airport is about 30 miles (around 45 minutes) and Glasgow Prestwick about 40 miles. If you’re not hiring a car, call us and we’ll talk through the options for the last leg.

Can I walk to Fir Park for the match?

Fir Park is about a mile from the hotel, so it’s walkable. If you’d rather not walk, ask us and we’ll suggest a local taxi firm. If you’re driving, our car park is free, but allow extra time for match-day traffic.

How do I get into Glasgow for a concert or a game?

Take the train from Motherwell to Glasgow Central and carry on from there. The OVO Hydro, Hampden Park and Celtic Park are all within about 15 miles of the hotel. Before you go, check the time of the last train back to Motherwell, and ask us about a taxi if you expect to be later.

I’m arriving before 2pm. Can I check in early?

Rooms are ready from 2pm. If you’re going to be early, or very late, call us in advance on {{PHONE}} and we’ll tell you what we can arrange on the day.
