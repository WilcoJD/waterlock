The Back o’ Barns room

A flexible, air-conditioned function suite

Our function suite, the Back o’ Barns room, can accommodate up to 120 delegates, depending on the layout you need.

It is comfortable and air-conditioned, and can be set up for presentations, training, dinners, dances and receptions. For smaller groups, our conservatory offers a bright alternative beside the house.

Our own kitchen does all the catering, from tea, coffee and lunches for a working day to a full dinner for a celebration. If your guests need to stay, we have 18 en-suite bedrooms.

On this page you’ll find how we set the room out for meetings and conferences, what we can arrange for the day, and how we look after celebrations, funeral receptions and community events. If you’d rather just talk it through, call us on {{PHONE}}.

What the room suits

The suite works well for team meetings, away days, interviews, training courses, AGMs and smaller conferences. It’s about 5 minutes’ walk from Motherwell station and has free on-site parking, so a team gathered from across the central belt can reach it easily by train or car.

If you need a second space, ask us about the conservatory for a breakout group or a smaller meeting running alongside the main one.

Tell us what you plan to bring, such as a laptop or flipcharts, and what you’d like help with, and we’ll talk through what’s possible in the room before the day.

Funeral receptions & memorial gatherings

A quiet room and a warm welcome after a service

We know a funeral tea is usually arranged at short notice, at a hard time. One call is enough to start, and you don’t need to decide everything at once.

We’ll set aside a private room for your family and friends, serve a warm meal or a buffet, and look after your guests so that you can be with the people who matter. If the numbers change on the day, tell us and we’ll adjust.

If you would rather, your funeral director can make the arrangements with us on your behalf. We’re also here for memorial gatherings held some time after the funeral, and for marking the anniversary of a loss.

Planning your event

From first call to the day itself

You don’t need everything decided before you get in touch. We’ll work through the details with you, in the order that suits you.

For a meeting, the whole process can happen over a couple of phone calls. For a big celebration, most people like to see the room first and then settle the menu and the running order over the following weeks.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Send us the kind of event, your date (or a few dates that would work) and rough numbers. We’ll check the diary and come back to you with availability.

  2. 2

    Talk it through

    We’ll go over the layout, catering, timings and any bedrooms you need, then send you a written quote and explain how to hold the date.

  3. 3

    Come and see the room

    View the function suite and the conservatory, walk through how the day will run, and ask us anything you like.

  4. 4

    Confirm the details

    Final numbers, menu choices, allergies and dietary needs, the table plan or layout, your running order, and arrival times for anyone you’re bringing, such as a speaker, a DJ or a band. We’ll confirm it all in writing.

  5. 5

    On the day

    We set the room out, welcome your guests, serve the food and look after the running of the day, resetting the room between sessions if you need us to.

Function questions

How many people can the function suite hold?

Up to 120 theatre-style or for a standing reception, and up to 100 for a seated dinner. For boardroom, classroom, U-shape and cabaret layouts the number depends on the tables and spacing you want, so tell us how many are coming and we’ll confirm what fits. The conservatory suits smaller groups.

Can we book the room for a few hours, an evening or several days?

Tell us the hours you need, including time to set up and clear away, and we’ll let you know what’s possible on your date and quote for it. A half-day meeting, an evening party and a two-day training course are all set out and priced differently.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your numbers, the catering you choose and the hours you need, so we quote for each event rather than publishing prices. Send us the basics using the checklist above and we’ll put a written quote together for you.

Who does the catering, and can you cater for dietary requirements?

Our own kitchen does the catering for events at the hotel, from tea and coffee to a full dinner. Tell us about allergies, intolerances and dietary requirements as early as you can and we’ll plan the menu around them. If you’d like to bring a celebration cake, just let us know.

Can delegates and guests stay overnight?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite bedrooms: singles, twins, doubles and family rooms sleeping up to 3. Tell us roughly how many rooms you need when you enquire and we’ll explain how to reserve them and how your guests can confirm their own bookings. Each booking confirmation shows whether breakfast is included.

Is the function suite accessible?

The hotel is a Victorian house, so please tell us about any access needs when you enquire. We’ll talk you through the route into the building and the function suite, and what we can do to make the day easier for your guests.

Can we have a bar and a late finish?

We can arrange a bar for an evening event in the suite. Ask us about a late licence when you enquire and we’ll tell you what’s possible on your date.

How much notice do you need?

As much as you can give us, especially for Saturdays and other popular dates. That said, funeral teas and small meetings are often arranged at short notice, so if your date is close, call us on {{PHONE}} rather than assuming we’re full.
