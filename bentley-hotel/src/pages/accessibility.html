Accessibility

Accessibility at Bentley Hotel

This page covers two things: how we have built this website so that as many people as possible can use it, and what to expect from the hotel building itself, a Victorian villa built as a private house, where many of the bedrooms are reached by stairs.

This website

Using this website

We want everyone to be able to find a room, check our prices and get in touch, whatever device or assistive technology they use. The site is built as simple, well-structured pages, with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA as our target. We haven’t had it independently audited, so if something gets in your way, please tell us and we will fix what we can.

{{icon:user}}

Keyboard and screen readers

Every page can be used with a keyboard alone. Tab moves through the links, buttons and form fields, and a “Skip to content” link at the top of each page jumps past the menu. Pages use proper headings, landmarks and form labels, so a screen reader can read them in order and announce what each control does.

{{icon:check}}

Visible focus

Whatever you have reached with the keyboard is marked with a clear outline, so you can always see where you are on the page. Menus, the photo viewer and the booking forms all show it.

{{icon:sun}}

Colour and contrast

Text and background colours are chosen for contrast, in the dark green bands as well as on the cream pages. We don’t rely on colour alone: the availability calendar, for example, says in words whether a room is free, down to its last room or fully booked.

{{icon:tv}}

Text size and zoom

Text is set in relative sizes, so it grows when you enlarge the text in your browser or zoom the page. The layout is designed to reflow to fit a phone screen or a zoomed window without sideways scrolling; wide tables and the availability calendar scroll within their own frame instead.

{{icon:clock}}

Reduced motion

Movement on the site is kept to a minimum. If your device or browser is set to reduce motion, the site switches off its animations and smooth scrolling and shows everything at once.

{{icon:wifi}}

Works without JavaScript

Every page reads and works with JavaScript turned off. The two exceptions are the live booking engine and the availability calendar. Each shows a plain alternative in its place: a link to our secure booking page, a simple date form, and our phone number.

Pictures and words

Every photograph that carries information has a text alternative describing what it shows, so a screen reader user knows what is in the picture. Where a picture only repeats what the words beside it already say, such as the room photo inside a card that names the room, it is marked so that screen readers skip it. The small icons beside headings and buttons are decorative and hidden from screen readers; the words beside them carry the meaning. We write in plain English, keep paragraphs short, and say when we don’t know something rather than papering over it.

Forms and feedback

Every form field has a visible label, and optional fields are marked as optional, so you know which ones you need to fill in. When something happens that you can’t see, such as the calendar loading new dates or the enquiry form handing over to your email app, the page announces it so that screen reader users hear it too.

Known limitations

Where you might have difficulty

Some parts of the site are provided by other companies, and a few are more complex than a page of text. Here is what to expect, and what to do instead if any of them gives you trouble.

The booking engine
The live booking form on our booking page is provided by Cloudbeds, the company that runs our reservations system. We don’t control its code. If you find it hard to use, you can book on our secure Cloudbeds booking page instead, or call us and we will take the booking over the phone.
The map
The map on our location page is embedded from OpenStreetMap, and its controls are theirs rather than ours. Everything the map shows is also given in text on the same page: our address, how to find us by train and car, and what is nearby.
The availability calendar
Our calendar is a table of rooms and nights. It has a caption and row and column headings, and you can move around it with the arrow keys, using Home and End to jump to the start and end of a row. Each night is announced with the room, the date, whether it is free and the lowest rate. On a small screen the table scrolls sideways within its own frame. If you would rather not use it, the booking search form on our rooms page does the same job, and so do we on the phone.
The photo viewer
On the gallery page, choosing a photo opens it in a larger view. Escape closes it, the arrow keys move between photos, and focus returns to where you were. With JavaScript off, the photo simply opens on its own.
Documents on other sites
Our history page links to North Lanarkshire Council’s conservation area appraisal, which is a PDF on the council’s website. We can’t vouch for its accessibility, but the facts we draw from it are set out in text on our page.

If you have trouble, call us

Nothing on this website needs to be done online. You can book a room, reserve a table or arrange an event by calling {{PHONE}}, and we will read out prices and availability for your dates.

If you would like any of the information on this site in another form, such as read over the phone or sent by email in plain text, just ask.

Tell us about a problem

If you can’t use part of this website, or something on it doesn’t work with your screen reader, magnifier or other assistive technology, we would like to know. Under the Equality Act 2010 we have a duty to make reasonable adjustments for disabled guests, and we would far rather hear about a problem than have you struggle with it.

The hotel building

Access at the hotel

Bentley Hotel is a Victorian house, built in 1874 as a private home, and many of our bedrooms are reached by stairs.

We would far rather talk this through with you before you book than have you arrive to a surprise. Please call us on {{PHONE}}, tell us what you need, and we will talk you through the most suitable room for your dates, the way into the building, and the ground-floor spaces.

If you would rather write, send us an enquiry with your dates and what you need, and we will reply with the same straight answer.

Questions we are happy to answer on the phone

These are the things guests ask us most often. Because the honest answer depends on which room or space you have in mind, we answer them for your visit rather than in general terms here.

  • Is there a lift, and which rooms are up stairs?
  • Is there a step-free way into the building?
  • Is there an accessible toilet?
  • Is there a hearing loop?
  • Does the room have a bath or a walk-in shower?
  • How far is the car park from the door?
  • Which rooms are nearest the entrance and the stairs?
  • Can you cater for allergies and dietary needs?

Assistance animals

Assistance animals are welcome. Other pets can’t stay with us. Please mention your assistance animal when you book so that we are ready for you both.

{{icon:car}}

Parking

There is a free car park on site. If you need to park as close to the door as possible, or need help with luggage, tell us when you book or when you arrive.

{{icon:info}}

Coming to a wedding or a function? If you or any of your guests have access needs, tell the person organising the event, or tell us directly, and we will plan the room layout and the route in with that in mind.
