Families

Staying with children

Our family room has a double bed and a single bed and sleeps up to three guests, one of whom may be a child, so a couple with a child can share one room. Breakfast is cooked to order, and children are welcome in the restaurant.

If you’re bringing a baby, or hoping for an extra bed, please speak to us before you book. No two rooms are quite the same shape, so we’d rather talk it through with you first than have you arrive to a surprise.

A bigger family? Book two rooms, mention it when you book, and we’ll do our best to place them near each other.

Good to know

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2pm and check-out is by 11am. If you’d like to check in earlier or check out later, please ask and we’ll help if we can. Arriving late in the evening? Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

Is breakfast included?

It depends on the rate you book. Your booking confirmation shows whether breakfast is included in your stay. If you’re not sure, call us on {{PHONE}} before you travel and we’ll check for you.

Can I pay on arrival?

Your booking confirmation explains the payment and cancellation terms for your rate, including when payment is taken. If anything in it is unclear, call us before your stay and we’ll talk it through.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is available on site for hotel guests.

Do the rooms have hairdryers and irons?

Ask us when you book and we’ll tell you what’s in your room and what we can bring up for you. Every room has a TV and tea and coffee making facilities.

Do you accept pets?

We’re sorry, but pets can’t stay at the hotel. Assistance animals are welcome.

Can I smoke in the hotel?

The Bentley is a non-smoking hotel. Smoking isn’t permitted in bedrooms or public areas.

Is there a minimum age to check in?

The guest checking in must be 18 or over.

Is there a lift, and is the hotel accessible?

The Bentley is a Victorian house, and many of our bedrooms are reached by stairs. If stairs are a concern, or anyone in your party has access needs, please call us on {{PHONE}} before booking. We’ll tell you honestly what we can offer for your dates, including any ground-floor options, and help you find the most suitable room. There’s more on our accessibility page.

Why book direct?

Booking with us directly means you’re dealing with the hotel itself, so you can ask us anything about your stay before you arrive. Use our online booking or call {{PHONE}}.
