Welcome to Bentley Hotel

A touch of comfort after a long day

Bentley Hotel sits in a historic building once known as Motherwell House. It was built in 1874 and has been home to many of Motherwell’s founding families.

The story handed down with the house is that it was built for Colonel Black of the Cameronians. Its sandstone exterior has kept its Victorian character, and inside, alongside the bedrooms, you’ll find our restaurant, a conservatory and a bar.

We’re on High Road, inside Motherwell’s Hamilton Road Conservation Area, next door to the North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre and a short walk from Motherwell station. That makes us an easy base for work, a match at Fir Park, a family occasion, or a few days exploring Glasgow, Edinburgh and the Clyde Valley.

Getting here

Easy to reach by rail and road

Glasgow is about 12 miles away and Edinburgh about 30, with the M74 and M8 motorways close by.

{{icon:train}}

By train

Motherwell station is about a 5-minute walk away, with direct trains to Glasgow, Edinburgh and London.

{{icon:road}}

By car

Close to junction 6 of the M74, with the M8 a few miles north. Free parking on site.

{{icon:plane}}

By air

Glasgow Airport is roughly 30 minutes by car and Edinburgh Airport about 45, traffic permitting.

{{icon:tree}}

On the doorstep

The Heritage Centre is next door. Strathclyde Country Park, M&D’s and Fir Park are all about 2 miles.
