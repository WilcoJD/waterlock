Rooms at The Bentley

Eighteen en-suite bedrooms in a Victorian house, five minutes’ walk from Motherwell station.

In every room

Every room has an en-suite bathroom, a TV, and tea and coffee making facilities. WiFi is free throughout, and the whole hotel is non-smoking.

Room layouts vary from one room to the next, as you’d expect in a house built in 1874. If you need a particular bed set-up, mention it when you book or give us a call and we’ll do our best to help.

Good to know

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2pm and check-out is by 11am. If you’d like to check in earlier or check out later, please ask and we’ll help if we can. Arriving late in the evening? Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is available on site for hotel guests.

Do you accept pets?

We’re sorry, but pets can’t stay at the hotel. Assistance animals are welcome.

Can I smoke in the hotel?

The Bentley is a non-smoking hotel. Smoking isn’t permitted in bedrooms or public areas.

Is there a minimum age to check in?

The guest checking in must be 18 or over.

Is the hotel accessible?

The Bentley is a Victorian house, and many bedrooms are reached by stairs. If you or anyone in your party has access needs, please call us on 01698 265588 before booking and we’ll help you find the most suitable room.

Why book direct?

Booking with us directly means you’re dealing with the hotel itself, so you can ask us anything about your stay before you arrive. Use our online booking or call 01698 265588.
