The venue

Everything in one place, from the ceremony to breakfast the next morning

Bentley Hotel is a venue for both the ceremony and the reception, for wedding parties of any size from 4 to 100 guests, with evening receptions for up to 120. The house is a sandstone villa built in 1874 at the west end of Motherwell. Guests reach it easily: Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk and there is free parking on site.

For the wedding breakfast, our function suite is comfortable, air-conditioned and exclusively yours, seating up to 100 for dinner. In the evening it opens up for a reception of up to 120, with a late licence until 1am. For a smaller wedding, the conservatory beside the restaurant is a bright, more intimate room for a few close family and friends.

Our own kitchen prepares your wedding meal. Choose from our range of menus or ask us to design something around you, and we’ll talk through allergies, dietary needs and children’s meals as we plan it. An overnight stay for the couple is part of our package, so when the last guests leave you are already home for the night.

Family and friends can stay too, in our 18 en-suite bedrooms, and a full Scottish breakfast cooked to order the next morning gives everyone time to catch up before they head off.

Our wedding package includes

  • An overnight stay in the hotel for the couple
  • Fresh floral arrangements for all guest tables
  • Personalised menus and table plan
  • Use of our wedding cake stand and knife
  • A late licence until 1am

Packages and prices are tailored to each wedding. Send an enquiry for current details.

Wedding questions

What couples ask before they visit

How many guests can you host?

Wedding parties of 4 to 100 guests, and evening receptions of up to 120. For smaller weddings the conservatory is ideal; for larger ones the air-conditioned function suite is exclusively yours.

Can we hold the ceremony at the hotel?

Yes, we host both the ceremony and the reception. Tell us what kind of ceremony you have in mind when you enquire and we’ll talk you through where in the house it could take place and what needs arranging in advance.

How much does a wedding at Bentley Hotel cost?

Every wedding is different, so we price each one individually. Tell us your date, your numbers and which parts of the day you’d like to hold with us, and we’ll come back with current prices and what is included. We’ll explain deposits and payment dates when you book.

Can our guests stay the night?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite bedrooms, from singles to a family room for three. Tell us roughly how many guests expect to stay and we’ll explain how to reserve rooms for them. Guests can also check availability and book directly.

Can we come and see the venue before we decide?

Of course. Call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry and we’ll arrange a time to show you round and talk through your plans.
