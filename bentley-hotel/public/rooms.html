Families

Staying with children

Our family room has a double bed and a single bed and sleeps up to three guests, one of whom may be a child, so a couple with a child can share one room. Breakfast is cooked to order, and children are welcome in the restaurant.

If you’re bringing a baby, or hoping for an extra bed, please speak to us before you book. No two rooms are quite the same shape, so we’d rather talk it through with you first than have you arrive to a surprise.

A bigger family? Book two rooms, mention it when you book, and we’ll do our best to place them near each other.

Things to do with children nearby
  • Family room: a double and a single bed, sleeping up to 3

Working in Motherwell?

A practical base for contractors and business travellers

Whether you’re on site in Lanarkshire, in meetings in Glasgow or Edinburgh, or passing through on the M74, The Bentley keeps a working stay simple.

Free parking on site

Park at the hotel for free for the length of your stay. There’s nothing to pay and no permit to arrange.

Free WiFi

Free WiFi throughout the hotel, for emails, calls home and the next day’s paperwork.

A cooked breakfast

A full Scottish breakfast, cooked to order, before you head out.

5 minutes from the station

Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk. The fastest trains reach Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes, and there are services to Edinburgh too.

Close to the M74

Junction 6 of the M74 (A723) is nearby, with the M8 a few miles north, so sites across Lanarkshire and the central belt are an easy drive.

Dinner without driving

Our restaurant and bar are under the same roof. Opening times vary, so see the restaurant page or call ahead.

Weekly stays and team bookings

Staying for a week or longer, or booking rooms for a team? Call us to arrange it. We’ll talk through dates and room types with you, and single and twin rooms work well for colleagues on the same job.

Your stay

The practical details

A few things worth knowing before you arrive. If your question isn’t answered here, call us on 01698 265588 and we’ll sort it out.

Arriving

Check-in is from 2pm. Please let us know your arrival time in advance, especially if you’ll be late in the evening, so someone is here to welcome you and hand over your key.

During your stay

Tell us if you need anything during your stay, whether it’s something for your room or a question about the area, and we’ll do our best to help.

Luggage

Arriving before 2pm, or leaving after 11am? Ask us about leaving your bags with us while you make the most of the day.

Smoking

The Bentley is a non-smoking hotel throughout, in the bedrooms and in all public areas.

Pets and assistance animals

Pets can’t stay at the hotel, but assistance animals are welcome. Let us know when you book so we can be ready for you.

Checking out

Check-out is by 11am. If you’d like a later check-out, ask and we’ll help if we can. Payment and cancellation terms depend on the rate you book and are set out in your confirmation.

A few more questions

Is breakfast included?

It depends on the rate you book. Your booking confirmation shows whether breakfast is included in your stay. If you’re not sure, call us on 01698 265588 before you travel and we’ll check for you.

Can I pay on arrival?

Your booking confirmation explains the payment and cancellation terms for your rate, including when payment is taken. If anything in it is unclear, call us before your stay and we’ll talk it through.

Do the rooms have hairdryers and irons?

Ask us when you book and we’ll tell you what’s in your room and what we can bring up for you. Every room has a TV and tea and coffee making facilities.

Is there a lift, and is the hotel accessible?

The Bentley is a Victorian house, and many of our bedrooms are reached by stairs. If stairs are a concern, or anyone in your party has access needs, please call us on 01698 265588 before booking. We’ll tell you honestly what we can offer for your dates, including any ground-floor options, and help you find the most suitable room. There’s more on our accessibility page.

Why book direct?

Booking with us directly means you’re dealing with the hotel itself, so you can ask us anything about your stay before you arrive. Use our online booking or call 01698 265588.
