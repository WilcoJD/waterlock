Why groups choose us

Everyone under one roof, and someone to arrange it

Bringing a group anywhere takes organising, and it’s easier when you can talk it through with the hotel. Call us with your dates and numbers and we’ll work out which of our 18 en-suite rooms suit your party: singles, twins for friends or team-mates, doubles for couples and a family room sleeping up to three.

Wedding parties take rooms for the night of the celebration, whether the wedding is here or elsewhere in Lanarkshire. Teams and supporters stay for fixtures at Fir Park, about a mile away, or for events at Ravenscraig and in Glasgow, where the OVO Hydro, Hampden and Celtic Park are within about 15 miles. Clubs can combine a meal here with a night’s stay, and families gather for birthdays, anniversaries and reunions.

If your group wants to eat together, our function suite, the Back o’ Barns room, seats up to 100 for dinner and holds up to 120 for a reception, with catering from our own kitchen. For a smaller party, ask about the conservatory or private dining in the restaurant. Breakfast the next morning is a full Scottish, cooked to order.

Getting everyone here is straightforward: free parking on site, Motherwell station about 5 minutes’ walk and junction 6 of the M74 nearby. Every room has a TV, tea and coffee making facilities and free WiFi.

Because the house dates from 1874, the rooms vary in shape and size, so tell us who needs to be near whom, and about any access needs, and we’ll do our best to place you well.

Questions

Group stays: what organisers ask us

How many rooms do you have?

Eighteen en-suite bedrooms: single, twin, double and family. If your party needs most of them, call us as early as you can.

How do I book several rooms?

A room or two can be booked online. For several, call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry with your dates and numbers, and we’ll hold the rooms together and explain how each guest confirms theirs.

Do you ask for a deposit?

Arrangements for holding rooms, confirming them and paying depend on the size of the group and the dates. We’ll explain them clearly when you book, so there are no surprises.

Can we eat together?

Yes. The function suite seats up to 100 for dinner, and the restaurant and conservatory suit smaller parties. Tell us about dietary needs and we’ll plan the menu with you.

Is there parking for a minibus or coach?

Parking on site is free for guests. If you’re arriving by minibus or coach, call us first so we can tell you what will fit and where best to drop off.
