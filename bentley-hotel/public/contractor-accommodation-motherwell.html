Why contractors stay with us

A practical base, without the fuss

If you’re working on a site in Lanarkshire, you want somewhere straightforward: a clean en-suite room, somewhere to park, WiFi that works, a proper breakfast and a short drive to the job. That is what Bentley Hotel is for.

We’re at the west end of Motherwell, close to junction 6 of the M74 (A723), with the M8 a few miles north. Ravenscraig is a short drive, and so are Hamilton, Wishaw, Bellshill and the rest of North and South Lanarkshire. Glasgow is about 12 miles away and Edinburgh about 30, so if the job moves, you don’t have to.

Our single rooms suit one person on their own; twins, with two single beds, suit two colleagues sharing. Every room has an en-suite bathroom, a TV, tea and coffee making facilities and free WiFi, and the hotel is non-smoking throughout.

At the end of the day the restaurant and bar are under the same roof, so you can eat without getting back in the van. In the morning, breakfast is a full Scottish, cooked to order.

For stays of a week or longer, or several rooms for a crew, call us on 01698 265588 and we’ll arrange dates, rooms and payment with you.

Questions

Working stays: what contractors ask us

Can we book rooms for a whole crew?

Yes. Call us on 01698 265588 with your dates and how many people are travelling, and we’ll talk through single and twin rooms and keep them together where we can. We have 18 rooms in all.

Do you do weekly or longer stays?

We do. For a week or longer it’s easiest to call us, so we can agree the dates, the rooms and how you’d like to pay before you arrive.

Is there parking for vans?

Guests park on site for free. If you’re bringing a larger van, or a vehicle with a trailer, give us a call first so we can tell you what fits.

Can I get breakfast early?

Breakfast is a full Scottish, cooked to order. If you need to be on site early, tell us when you book and we’ll let you know what we can do about your start time. Your confirmation shows whether breakfast is included in your rate.

What time can I check in, and what if I'm late?

Check-in is from 2pm and check-out is by 11am. If you’ll be arriving late in the evening after a shift, please let us know in advance. The main guest checking in must be 18 or over.
