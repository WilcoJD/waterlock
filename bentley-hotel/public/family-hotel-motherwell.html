Staying with children

A family room, a cooked breakfast and plenty to do nearby

Our family room has a double bed and a single bed and sleeps up to three guests, one of whom may be a child, so a couple with a child can share one room. Bigger family? Book two rooms, mention it when you book, and we’ll do our best to put them near each other.

Children are welcome in the restaurant, and when breakfast is part of your rate it is a full Scottish breakfast cooked to order. If you are bringing a baby, or hoping for a cot or an extra bed, please speak to us before you book. No two rooms in a house built in 1874 are quite the same shape, and we’d rather talk it through first.

Then there is what is outside the door. The North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre is next door and free to visit, with a viewing tower over the Clyde Valley. Strathclyde Country Park is about a mile away, with its loch, woodland walks and watersports, and on its edge, about 2 miles from us, are M&D’s theme park and Amazonia, the indoor tropical house. About a mile the other way, Dalzell Estate and the RSPB reserve at Baron’s Haugh are good for a walk with a picnic. For a city day, the fastest trains from Motherwell station, 5 minutes’ walk away, reach Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes.

Parking is free on site, so unloading the car with children and bags is simple, and the whole hotel is non-smoking.

Questions

Family stays: what parents ask us

How many people does the family room sleep?

Up to three, one of whom may be a child. It has a double bed and a single bed, and like every room it is en suite with a TV, tea and coffee and free WiFi.

Can you provide a cot or an extra bed?

Please call us on 01698 265588 before you book. Our rooms vary in shape and size, so we’d rather talk through what works for your family first.

We are a family of four or more. What are our options?

Book two rooms, for example the family room and a twin, and mention that you are travelling together. We’ll do our best to place them near each other.

Is breakfast included, and can the children eat with us?

Whether breakfast is included depends on your rate, and your confirmation will say. Children are welcome in the restaurant, and breakfast is a full Scottish, cooked to order.

What is there for children to do nearby?

The North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre is next door and free to visit. Strathclyde Country Park is about a mile away, M&D’s theme park and Amazonia about 2 miles, and Dalzell Estate and RSPB Baron’s Haugh about a mile the other way.
