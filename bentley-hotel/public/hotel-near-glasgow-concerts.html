Gigs, shows and big matches

Twelve miles from Glasgow, with the train doing the hard work

Glasgow’s biggest venues are not far from Motherwell. The OVO Hydro, Hampden Park and Celtic Park are all within about 15 miles of Bentley Hotel, and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk from our door. The fastest trains reach Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes, so you can leave the car in our free car park and let someone else do the driving.

Staying out here has its advantages: free parking rather than a hunt for a space, a bed in a Victorian house at the west end of town, and the restaurant and bar under the same roof if you want to eat before you go. In the morning there is a full Scottish breakfast cooked to order, if it is part of your rate, and no city traffic to get out of.

Two practical points. Check the time of the last train back to Motherwell before you go, or arrange a taxi for after the show. And if you expect to get back to the hotel late, please tell us when you book or call us on the day.

Doubles suit couples, twins suit friends going to a gig together, and singles are a sensible choice if you are travelling alone.

Questions

Getting to the show and back

How do we get from the hotel to the OVO Hydro?

Take the train from Motherwell station, about 5 minutes’ walk from the hotel, to Glasgow Central, then continue to the venue by local train, bus or taxi.

Will we be able to get back to Motherwell after the show?

Check the time of the last train back to Motherwell before you set off, and if the show may finish after it, arrange a taxi. Please tell us when you book if you expect to arrive back late.

Can we park at the hotel and leave the car while we are in Glasgow?

Yes. Parking is free for guests for the whole of your stay, from check-in until you head home.

Can we eat at the hotel before we go?

Our restaurant and bar are under the same roof. Opening times vary, so call us on 01698 265588 before you travel to check the times and ask about a table.

Is breakfast included the morning after?

That depends on the rate you book. Your confirmation should say, and we can tell you if you are unsure. Where it is included, breakfast is a full Scottish, cooked to order in our kitchen. Ask when you book if you’d like the breakfast times for your stay.
