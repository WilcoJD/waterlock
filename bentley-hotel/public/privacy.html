Privacy & cookies

How we look after your information

This notice explains, in plain words, what happens to your details when you browse this website, send us an enquiry or book a room, and which cookies are and aren’t involved.

Last updated November 2026

Cookies Your rights

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser.
  • The enquiry form opens your own email app. Your message reaches us as an ordinary email, sent from your address.
  • When you book online, your details go into Cloudbeds, the system that runs our reservations and takes payment on our behalf.
  • The availability calendar asks our server which rooms are free. It sends the dates you are looking at, not personal details.
  • A few things on the site come from other companies: the typefaces, the map and the booking engine. Each may receive your IP address or set its own cookies.
  • You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it.

Who we are

Bentley Hotel, Motherwell

We are a small hotel at 19 High Road, Motherwell, with 18 bedrooms, a restaurant, a bar and a function suite. This website is ours, and this notice is about what we and the website do with your information.

In data protection terms, we are the ‘controller’ of the booking and enquiry details you give us. That simply means we decide what they are used for and we are responsible for looking after them. The law that applies is the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

If anything on this page is unclear, or you want to know something it doesn’t answer, call or email us and we’ll give you a straight answer.

What this website does

What happens when you use the site

This is a simple website. Most of it is pages you read. Only the enquiry form, the booking page and the availability calendar do anything with information at all, and here is exactly what each of them does.

Browsing the pages

The website is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare may keep standard server logs, such as the IP address of your device, the pages requested and the time, for security and to keep the site running. We don’t run analytics or tracking scripts of our own, so we don’t follow what you do on the site.

Sending an enquiry

The form on our contact page doesn’t send anything to a server. When you press Send, it opens your own email app with your enquiry already written out, and you send it to us from your own address as an ordinary email. We keep the email in our hotel mailbox while we deal with your enquiry.

Booking a room online

Online booking runs on Cloudbeds, the reservations system that manages our rooms. When you book on our booking page, the details you enter (your name, contact details, dates, any requests and your payment card) are entered into Cloudbeds, which processes bookings and payments on our behalf. We see your booking in our reservations diary and use it to look after your stay. Cloudbeds publishes its own privacy policy.

The availability calendar

The calendar asks our own server which rooms are free on which nights, and the lowest nightly rate. The request contains only the dates you are looking at. It sends no personal details, and we keep nothing from it. When you choose dates and press Book, they are carried to the booking page in the web address, along with the number of guests and the room you picked.

Calling or emailing us

If you book a room, a table or an event by phone or email, we note the details we need (your name, contact details, dates, numbers, and any dietary or access needs) in our reservations diary or our email, and use them only to arrange what you have asked for.

Links to other websites

Some links take you off this site: to Google Maps and OpenStreetMap for directions, to National Rail and ScotRail for train times, to the Food Standards Agency for our food hygiene result, to North Lanarkshire Council for the conservation area appraisal, and to Cloudbeds’ own booking page for the hotel. Each of those websites has its own privacy notice, and this one doesn’t cover them.

Cookies

Cookies on this website

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store, so that it can recognise you when you come back or move from page to page.

This website sets no cookies of its own. We have no analytics, advertising or social media cookies, and we don’t show a cookie banner because the site itself has nothing to ask your consent for. The booking engine, the fonts and the map come from other companies, and what they may set is described below.

The booking engine

When you use the booking page, Cloudbeds’ booking engine may set cookies that are needed to complete your booking, for example to remember your dates and room choice as you move through the steps. These are set by Cloudbeds, under its own privacy policy, and only on the pages where the booking engine appears.

Fonts and the map

Google Fonts and the OpenStreetMap map may use cookies or similar techniques of their own when your browser fetches content from them. The table above links to their notices.

Controlling cookies in your browser

Every modern browser lets you see which cookies are stored, delete them, and block new ones. You’ll usually find the controls under Settings, then Privacy (or Privacy and security). You can block cookies from particular sites or from all sites, set the browser to clear cookies each time you close it, or use a private browsing window, which discards cookies when it closes.

Bear in mind that blocking all cookies may stop the booking engine completing a booking. If that happens, you can always book by calling us on 01698 265588.

Your booking and enquiry details

What we use your details for

Whether you book online, by phone or by email, we use the details you give us for a short list of reasons and nothing else.

To provide your stay or event
Your name, contact details, dates, room or function details, numbers of guests, and any dietary or access needs you tell us about, so that we can have the right room or space ready and feed you properly. We need these to carry out our agreement with you.
To take payment
Payment for online bookings is taken through Cloudbeds’ booking engine, which processes it on our behalf. For events and bookings made with us directly, payment is arranged with you in person or by phone, and we keep a record of what was paid.
To contact you about it
To confirm your booking, answer your questions, let you know about anything that affects your stay or event, and deal with anything you raise afterwards.
To meet our legal and accounting obligations
We must keep records of bookings and payments for tax and accounting purposes. Hotels in the UK are also required by law to keep a register of guests, with names and nationalities, and a few more details for some guests from overseas, which the police may inspect.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your details to anyone.
  • We don’t add you to a marketing list because you booked or enquired. If we ever want to send you news or offers, we will ask you first.
  • We don’t make automated decisions about you or build profiles of our guests.
  • We share your details only with those who need them to provide your stay or event, such as Cloudbeds, which runs our reservations and takes online payments, and with the authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep your details

We keep booking and enquiry details for as long as we need them to arrange and provide your stay or event, and to deal with anything that arises from it afterwards.

After that, we keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as records of bookings and payments for tax and accounting purposes and the guest register, for as long as those rules say we must. We delete the rest once we no longer need it.

An enquiry that doesn’t lead to a booking is deleted once it has been dealt with and we have no further reason to keep it. Details held in Cloudbeds are kept under its own retention rules as well as ours.

Want something deleted sooner? Ask us. Unless the law says we have to keep it, we will delete it.

Your rights

Your rights over your information

UK data protection law gives you a set of rights over the details we hold about you. You can use any of them by calling, emailing or writing to us. There is normally no charge, and we will reply within one month, as the law requires. We may ask you to confirm who you are first, so that we don’t hand your details to someone else.

Access

Ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, and an explanation of what we use them for.

Correction

Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or to complete anything that is missing.

Erasure

Ask us to delete your details where we no longer have a reason to keep them.

Restriction

Ask us to stop using your details, but keep them, while a question about them is being sorted out.

Objection

Object to our using your details where we rely on our own legitimate interests rather than on our agreement with you.

Portability

Ask for the details you gave us in a common electronic format, so that you can take them elsewhere.

If you are unhappy

If you think we have handled your information badly, please tell us first and we will do our best to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s independent regulator for data protection, at ico.org.uk.

A few other things worth knowing

Security

This website and the booking engine are served over an encrypted connection (https), so what you type cannot be read on its way to us. Your booking details are held in Cloudbeds, our reservations system, which is built for exactly this purpose.

Children

This website is not aimed at children, and the guest checking in must be 18 or over. The only details we hold about children are those an adult gives us as part of a family booking, such as how many children are staying.

Changes to this notice

If we change what the website does, for example by adding a new booking tool or any kind of analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top. The notice was last updated in November 2026.
