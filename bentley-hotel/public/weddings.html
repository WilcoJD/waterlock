Congratulations

Your day, the way you want it

All of us at The Bentley would like to congratulate you on your forthcoming wedding and wish you the very best for your future together.

The Bentley Hotel is an ideal venue for both the ceremony and the reception. Our function suite offers comfortable, air-conditioned private dining, and our conservatory suits a smaller wedding party.

We can offer an extensive choice of services and menus, or our experienced team can design your wedding around your own needs and preferences. We understand that your day deserves to be the very best, and we’d be delighted to share in your celebrations and make sure everything runs smoothly.

Wedding questions

Can we have our ceremony and reception at The Bentley?

Yes. The Bentley is a venue for both the ceremony and the reception. Get in touch with your date and we’ll talk you through how the day can run.

How many guests can you host?

We can host wedding parties of 4 to 100 guests, and evening receptions of up to 120. For smaller weddings, our conservatory is ideal.

Can our guests stay at the hotel?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite bedrooms, from singles to family rooms. Ask us about reserving rooms for your wedding guests when you enquire.

How late can the celebrations go on?

Our wedding package includes a late licence until 1am.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Our kitchen can talk through allergies and dietary requirements for you and your guests when we plan your menu.

Let’s talk about your wedding

Tell us your date and guest numbers, and we’ll be in touch with availability and details.
