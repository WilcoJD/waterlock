The house

Motherwell House, from the High Road

Bentley Hotel began as Motherwell House, a family home of 1874 which, as the story goes, was built for Colonel Black of the Cameronians. Much has changed inside since then, but the outside has kept its character: warm sandstone, a tall square tower, stepped gables and the bay windows you’ll find in some of the bedrooms.

The glass conservatory, on the left of the photograph, was purpose-built onto the side of the restaurant. The house stands among the Victorian villas of Motherwell’s west end, inside the Hamilton Road Conservation Area, about 5 minutes’ walk from the station.
