The day

Christmas Day at Bentley Hotel

Christmas Day is for being with the people you love, not for peeling potatoes at seven in the morning. On 25 December we lay the restaurant and the conservatory for lunch, with tables for families large and small and a festive menu cooked in our own kitchen, so all you have to do is turn up.

Each party has its own table, whether that is two of you or three generations, and the conservatory, with daylight through the glass, suits a long, unhurried lunch. Children are welcome and we can serve smaller portions. The menu keeps to what Christmas lunch should be, with a traditional main course and alternatives for those who want them, and it is confirmed each autumn along with the price and the sitting times. Tell us about allergies and dietary needs when you book.

Tables for Christmas Day are limited, so get in touch early. If family are travelling to join you, our 18 en-suite rooms are under the same roof: breakfast on Christmas morning, where it is part of your rate, then a short walk through to lunch. See our festive stays for rooms over the holidays.

Questions

Christmas Day lunch: what people ask us

How far ahead should we book Christmas Day lunch?

As early as you can: Christmas Day tables are limited. Call 01698 265588 or send an enquiry and we’ll note your interest and let you know as soon as this year’s menu and price are confirmed.

Is a deposit required?

A deposit may be needed to hold a Christmas Day table. We’ll explain the amount, the due date and our cancellation terms when you book, so there are no surprises.

Can you cater for allergies, and for vegetarian or vegan guests?

Yes. Tell us when you book about allergies, intolerances and anyone who is vegetarian or vegan, and how many children are coming so we can plan smaller portions.

Is there parking on Christmas Day, and do trains run?

Parking at the hotel is free. Trains are very limited on Christmas Day, so most guests come by car or stay with us the night before. Motherwell is just off junction 6 of the M74.
