The weekend

Easter at Bentley Hotel

Easter moves with the moon, so the long weekend lands anywhere from late March to late April. Whenever it falls, the pattern is the same: four days off, the first proper spring weather if we’re lucky, and families looking for somewhere to eat together and something to do with the children.

On Easter Sunday we serve a family lunch in the restaurant and the conservatory, cooked in our own kitchen, with a choice at each course. Children are welcome, and we can arrange something for them to do while the grown-ups finish their coffee; ask what we have planned this year. Larger family gatherings can take the function suite for private dining. The menu, the sittings and the price are confirmed each season.

If you’re staying, our 18 en-suite rooms include family rooms with a double and a single bed, sleeping up to three. Strathclyde Country Park is about a mile away, with the loch, woodland walks and the remains of a Roman bathhouse, and M&D’s theme park and Amazonia sit on its edge about 2 miles from the hotel. The North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre is next door and free to visit, and the Dalzell Estate and RSPB Baron’s Haugh are about a mile away for a quieter walk. Breakfast is cooked to order each morning, parking is free, and Glasgow Central is under 20 minutes by the fastest train from Motherwell station if you’d like a day in the city.

Questions

Easter: what people ask us

How do we book Easter Sunday lunch, or a room for the weekend?

Rooms can be booked online at any time. For lunch, call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry with your numbers and we’ll confirm this year’s menu, sittings and price and hold your table.

Do we need to pay a deposit?

For larger tables we may ask for a deposit to hold the booking; we’ll explain how much, when it is due and our cancellation terms when you book. For rooms, the payment and cancellation terms depend on the rate you choose and are set out in your confirmation.

Can you cater for dietary requirements and for children?

Yes. Tell us about allergies, intolerances and vegetarian or vegan guests when you book and the kitchen will plan for them. Children are welcome in the restaurant. If you’re bringing a baby or need a particular bed set-up, call us before booking a room so we can talk it through.

Is there parking, and can we manage without a car?

Parking at the hotel is free. Without a car you’ll still do fine: Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, the Heritage Centre is next door and Strathclyde Country Park is about a mile on foot.
