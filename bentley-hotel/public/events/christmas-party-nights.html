The night

Christmas party nights at Bentley Hotel

December is when Motherwell’s offices, clubs, teams and groups of friends gather to see the year out, and our function suite, the Back o’ Barns, is made for it. It seats up to 100 for dinner and holds up to 120 once the tables are cleared for dancing, and it is air-conditioned, which matters more than you’d think by the third song.

A party night here runs the way a good one should. Find your table, sit down to a festive dinner cooked in our own kitchen, and then the music starts and the floor fills. We can arrange the music, and we’ll explain the licence when you book so you know how late the night can run.

Smaller groups can ask about joining a shared party night, a good way for a team of eight or ten to have a big night out without hiring a room. Larger groups can ask about having the function suite to themselves. Getting home is simple: parking is free, Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, and our 18 en-suite rooms are in the hotel, with breakfast cooked to order the next morning where it is part of your rate.

Questions

Party nights: what organisers ask us

How do we book a Christmas party night?

Call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry with rough numbers and the dates that suit your group. We’ll confirm what is available and explain how to hold the booking.

Do we pay a deposit?

A deposit is likely to be needed to secure the date for your group; we’ll set out the amount, the due date and the cancellation terms when you book.

What about allergies and dietary requirements?

We’ll ask for menu choices from your group in advance, and that is the time to tell us about allergies, intolerances and vegetarian or vegan guests.

How late does the night go, and how do we get home?

We’ll explain the licence and the finishing time when you book. Parking is free, Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, and our rooms are a short walk from the dance floor if anyone would rather stay the night.
