The evening

St Andrew's Day at Bentley Hotel

St Andrew has been Scotland’s patron saint for centuries, and 30 November, his feast day, is the country’s national day. It is a quieter celebration than Burns Night or Hogmanay, which is part of its appeal: an evening for good Scottish food, a dram and a bit of music, without the speeches.

At Bentley Hotel the night is built around the kitchen. We serve a Scottish menu for the evening, drawing on the things the country does well, cooked in our own kitchen, with the dishes and the price confirmed each season. We raise a dram to the saint and the saltire. Tables range from two to a party of friends or a club night out, and for a larger group we can set the function suite for private dining and arrange live music or a ceilidh band.

St Andrew’s Day also opens the winter here. The festive season follows close behind, and Burns Night comes round in January, so if you’re looking for a night out with colleagues or a club before the Christmas rush, this is the one. Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk for anyone coming from Glasgow or Edinburgh, parking is free, and our 18 en-suite rooms are here if you’d rather stay and have breakfast with us.

Questions

St Andrew's Day: what people ask us

How do we book for St Andrew's Day?

Phone us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry and tell us how many you expect. We’ll give you this year’s date, menu and price and keep a table for you. For a club or works night, say roughly how many are coming and whether you’d like music.

Do we need to pay a deposit?

Group bookings and private hire of the function suite usually need a deposit. We’ll set out how much, when it is due and our cancellation terms before you confirm.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Tell us about allergies, intolerances and vegetarian or vegan guests when you book and the kitchen will plan a Scottish menu that works for everyone at the table.

Is there parking, and can we come by train?

Yes to both. Parking at the hotel is free, and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, with trains to Glasgow Central (under 20 minutes at the fastest) and to Edinburgh. Rather than travel late, book a room and stay the night.

Celebrate St Andrew's Day with us

Give us your numbers and we’ll hold a table, or the function suite for a group. Rooms can be booked online.
