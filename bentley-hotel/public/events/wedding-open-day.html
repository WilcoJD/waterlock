The day

What happens at an open day

A wedding venue is hard to judge from photographs. An open day lets you walk through it. We set the function suite as we would for a wedding breakfast, with the tables dressed, a table plan on display and fresh flowers on the guest tables, as they are in our wedding package. The conservatory is set for a smaller wedding party, and both rooms are open for as long as you want to look.

The team who plan and run our weddings are there to talk. Bring your date, or a few dates, and rough numbers for the day and the evening, and we’ll tell you honestly what would work. We host wedding parties of 4 to 100 guests and evening receptions of up to 120, with a late licence until 1am. Ask about ceremonies at the hotel, about the menus our own kitchen can offer or design around you, about rooms for the two of you and for your guests, and about what the package includes. There is no obligation, and nobody will follow you round with a clipboard.

Open day dates are confirmed each season and passed on to anyone who has sent us a wedding enquiry. If there isn’t one coming up, don’t wait for it: call us and we’ll show you round by appointment, with the same people and the same straight answers. Bentley Hotel is about 5 minutes’ walk from Motherwell station, with free parking on site, so it is an easy visit from Glasgow, Edinburgh or anywhere in Lanarkshire.

What to expect

The suite, the conservatory and the package

The function suite

Air-conditioned and exclusively yours on the day, seating up to 100 for the wedding breakfast and taking up to 120 for an evening reception. You'll see it set as it would be for you.

The conservatory

A bright glass room on the side of the house, ideal for a smaller wedding party or for welcome drinks while the photographs are taken.

Questions

Open days: what couples ask us

Do we need to register?

Yes please, so we know how many to expect and can give everyone time with the team. Send a wedding enquiry mentioning the open day, or call us on 01698 265588, and we’ll confirm the next date and times, or arrange a private viewing if that suits you better.

Can we hold a date on the day, and is a deposit needed?

Yes. If you find the date you want, we’ll explain how to hold it, what deposit is needed and when it is due, and our cancellation terms, before you commit to anything.

Can we talk about menus and dietary requirements?

Yes. The kitchen can offer a choice of menus or design one around you, and the open day is a good time to raise allergies, intolerances, vegetarian or vegan guests and children’s meals so we can plan for them from the start.

Is there parking, and can we come by train?

Both. There is free parking at the hotel, and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, with trains to Glasgow Central (under 20 minutes at the fastest) and to Edinburgh, which is handy for your guests on the day too. If you’re travelling a long way to see us, book a room and make a night of it.
