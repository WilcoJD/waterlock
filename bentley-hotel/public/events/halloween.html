The night

Halloween at Bentley Hotel

Halloween has deep roots in Scotland. Long before the pumpkins arrived, children here went guising in costume, performed a song or a joke for their sweets, carved lanterns out of neeps and dooked for apples in a basin of water. Some of that still happens on the streets of Motherwell every 31 October, and some of it can happen in our function suite.

For adults, we can set the suite for a party: decorated for the night, with a buffet or a sit-down meal from our own kitchen, a DJ or live music by arrangement, and room for up to 120 standing or 100 seated. It works for a group of friends, a works night out or a club that wants a room of its own, and fancy dress is encouraged but never compulsory. For families, we can arrange an afternoon earlier in the day with games, something to eat and a prize for the best costume. What we run each year, and what it costs, is confirmed each season.

The house itself helps. A sandstone villa built in 1874, with a tower and tall bay windows, needs very little decoration to look the part on a dark October night. The function suite is air-conditioned, parking is free and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, which matters at the end of a party. Our 18 en-suite rooms are here if you’d rather stay than wait for a taxi.

Questions

Halloween: what people ask us

How do we book, and can we hire the suite for our own party?

Give us a call on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry with your numbers and we’ll tell you what we have planned this year. If you’d rather have the function suite to yourselves, we’ll talk through dates, food and music for a private party.

Do we need to pay a deposit?

Private hire and most group bookings need a deposit to hold the date. We’ll tell you how much and when it is due, and go through our cancellation terms, before anything is confirmed.

Can you cater for dietary requirements, and is the family afternoon suitable for young children?

Yes. Give us a note of allergies, intolerances and vegetarian or vegan guests when you book and the kitchen will plan for them. When we run a family afternoon it is meant for younger children, with games pitched at them and nothing too frightening; ask us about ages when you enquire.

Is there parking, and can we come by train?

Parking at the hotel is free, and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk for trains back to Glasgow, under 20 minutes at the fastest. Late finishers can book a room and stay the night.

Plan your Halloween party

Tell us your numbers and whether you want to join our night or hire the suite for your own, and we’ll take it from there. Rooms can be booked online.
