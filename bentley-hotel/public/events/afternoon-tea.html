The occasion

Afternoon tea at Bentley Hotel

Afternoon tea has been a British habit since the 1840s, so the story goes, when dinner moved later and something was needed to fill the gap. The form hasn’t changed much since: finger sandwiches, scones with cream and jam, a few small cakes and a proper pot of tea, eaten slowly in the middle of the afternoon with people you like.

We serve it in the conservatory, the purpose-built glass room on the side of the house, which suits exactly this kind of unhurried sitting. It is laid by arrangement rather than every day, so we ask you to book ahead and tell us your numbers. The sandwiches, scones and cakes come from our own kitchen. What goes on the stand, and the price, are confirmed when you book, and we can add a glass of fizz, a birthday cake with candles, or a savoury tier for anyone who isn’t a cake person.

Afternoon tea suits the occasions that don’t need an evening: a birthday with the grandparents, a baby shower, a retirement, a christening after the service, a hen afternoon before the hen night, or a get-together of old friends. The conservatory takes smaller parties; for a crowd, the function suite seats up to 100 and can be dressed for the occasion. If anyone is travelling, our 18 en-suite rooms are here, parking is free and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk.

Questions

Afternoon tea: what people ask us

How do we book afternoon tea?

Afternoon tea is served by arrangement, so please book ahead. Call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry with your date, your numbers and the occasion, and we’ll confirm what we can do and the price.

Do we need to pay a deposit?

Sometimes, for larger parties. If a deposit is needed we’ll say how much and when it is due, and explain our cancellation terms, when you book.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Tell us about allergies, intolerances and gluten-free, vegetarian or vegan guests when you book and the kitchen will bake with them in mind. Children are welcome; ask us about a smaller stand for them.

Is there parking, and can we come by train?

Parking at the hotel is free, and Motherwell station, about 5 minutes’ walk away, has trains from Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes at the fastest. Relatives travelling for the occasion can book a room.
