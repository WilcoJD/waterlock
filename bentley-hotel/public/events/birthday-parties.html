The occasion

A birthday at Bentley Hotel

Eighteenth, twenty-first, fortieth, sixtieth or eightieth: the big birthdays are the ones where the whole family and the old friends turn up, some of them from a long way away. What the day needs is a room that fits the party, food that everyone will eat, somewhere to raise a glass, and beds nearby for the people who have travelled.

For a quiet celebration we lay a private table in the restaurant or the conservatory, where a family can sit down to dinner together and the cake arrives when you give the nod. For a party, our Back o’ Barns function suite holds up to 120 for a standing reception and seats 100 for dinner, and it is air-conditioned. Choose a sit-down meal or a buffet from our own kitchen, bring a DJ or a band if you’d like music, and let us run a bar for the evening. If you want the night to go on, ask us about a late licence when you book.

You’re welcome to dress the room with your own decorations; we’ll agree a time for setting up. And when the party is over, the birthday person and the guests who have come furthest can stay the night in one of our 18 en-suite rooms and have breakfast with us rather than driving home.

Questions

Birthday parties: what people ask us

How many people can the function suite take?

Up to 120 for a standing reception and 100 seated for dinner. For smaller numbers we’d suggest a private table in the restaurant or the conservatory instead, which feels more like a dinner than a function.

Can we have music, and how late can the party go?

Yes, you can bring a DJ or a band for the function suite; tell us what you have in mind when you book. The bar runs through the evening, and if you’d like the party to go on later, ask us about a late licence.

Can we bring a cake and decorations?

Yes to both. Bring the cake and we’ll set it out with a stand and a knife and bring it in when you say. Decorations are welcome too; we’ll agree a time for you to dress the room beforehand.

Do we need to pay a deposit?

We may ask for a deposit to hold the room and the date. We’ll explain how much, when it is due and our cancellation terms when you book, and confirm the menu and this season’s prices at the same time.

Plan the birthday

Tell us the date, the number of guests and whether you’d like a dinner or a party, and we’ll come back with what we can do.
