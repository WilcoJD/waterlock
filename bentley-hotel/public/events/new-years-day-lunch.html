The day

The first of January at Bentley Hotel

New Year’s Day asks for very little: good company, a decent lunch and no washing up. On 1 January we lay the restaurant and the conservatory for a slow, easy lunch, for families who gathered the night before, friends starting the year together, and guests who saw the bells in with us.

The pace is deliberately gentle. Tables are laid for two or for a dozen, nobody is hurried, and the conservatory, with daylight through the glass, is a pleasant place to sit on a short January afternoon. The menu is confirmed each autumn along with the price and the sitting times. Tell us about allergies, dietary needs and children when you book.

If you stayed with us for Hogmanay, lunch is the natural end to the stay: breakfast when you surface, where it is part of your rate, a walk to Strathclyde Country Park about a mile away to clear the head, then lunch before the drive or the train home. Check-out is by 11am, so ask about leaving your bags with us while you eat. Parking is free, and train services are reduced on 1 January, so check times before you travel.

Questions

New Year's Day lunch: what people ask us

How do we book a table for New Year's Day?

Call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry and tell us how many are coming. We’ll confirm this year’s menu, price and sitting times and hold your table.

Do we need to pay a deposit?

For larger tables we may ask for a deposit; we’ll tell you the amount, the due date and our cancellation terms when you book.

Can you cater for dietary requirements and children?

Yes. Tell us about allergies, intolerances and vegetarian or vegan guests when you book, and how many children are coming, and we’ll plan smaller portions for them.

Is there parking, and do trains run on New Year's Day?

Parking at the hotel is free. Train services are reduced on 1 January, so check times before you travel, or stay the night before and walk through to lunch.
