The evening

Valentine's Day at Bentley Hotel

Valentine’s Day doesn’t need a grand gesture. A table for two, food cooked with care and nobody looking at the clock will do. On 14 February our restaurant and conservatory are laid for couples, with a menu written for the night from our own kitchen, and the evening is yours to take as slowly as you like.

The conservatory is the room to ask for if you’d like a little more privacy: a purpose-built glass room at the side of the house, with candles on the tables. The restaurant itself is a little livelier, which some couples prefer. Tell us which you’d like when you book, and whether you’d like flowers on the table or a quiet corner. The menu and price are confirmed each season, and we’ll plan for allergies and dietary needs when you tell us.

Getting home has an easy answer. Our double rooms are upstairs, some with the bay windows that give the house its character, each with an en-suite bathroom, a TV, tea and coffee and free WiFi. Book a room with your table and the evening ends with a flight of stairs rather than a taxi, and begins again with breakfast cooked to order. Parking is free, and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk.

Questions

Valentine's dinner: what people ask us

How do we book a table for Valentine's Day?

Call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry. We’ll confirm this year’s menu and price, hold your table and note whether you’d prefer the restaurant or the conservatory. Tables go quickly, so book early.

Is there a deposit?

We may ask for a small deposit to hold a Valentine’s table. We’ll explain how much, when it is due and our cancellation terms when you book.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Tell us about allergies, intolerances and vegetarian or vegan preferences when you book, and the kitchen will plan for them.

Is there parking, and can we stay the night?

Parking at the hotel is free, and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk. Our double rooms are upstairs; check availability for 14 February and book your room alongside your table.

Book a table for two

Tell us you’re coming and we’ll hold your table for 14 February. A double room for the night can be booked online.
