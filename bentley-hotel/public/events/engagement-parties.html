The occasion

An engagement party at Bentley Hotel

An engagement party is often the first time the two families are in the same room, and the first time the couple’s friends meet each other’s. It is a lighter occasion than the wedding: drinks as people arrive, a toast to the couple, something good to eat, perhaps a few words from a parent, and the rest of the evening to talk. It sets the tone for everything that follows.

At Bentley Hotel the size of the party decides the room. For a gathering of family and close friends we lay the conservatory beside the restaurant, which suits a sit-down meal or a relaxed buffet. For a bigger celebration our Back o’ Barns function suite holds up to 120 for a standing reception and seats 100 for dinner, with a bar for the evening and space for music if you’d like it. Either way the food comes from our own kitchen and we agree it with you beforehand. If there is a cake, we’ll set it on a stand with a knife ready.

Guests who have travelled can stay in one of our 18 en-suite rooms and have breakfast with us in the morning; parking is free and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk. And if the party goes well, you’ll already know the venue. We hold weddings for 4 to 100 guests, with evening receptions of up to 120, and we’d be glad to show you round while you’re here.

Questions

Engagement parties: what people ask us

Which room suits an engagement party?

It depends on numbers. For family and close friends, the conservatory beside the restaurant suits a gathering of that size. For a larger party, the function suite gives you room to move, a bar and space for music, and holds up to 120 standing or 100 seated.

Do we need a sit-down meal?

No. Many engagement parties are drinks and a buffet, with people moving about and talking. Others sit down to dinner. Tell us which you’d prefer and we’ll agree a menu with you; this season’s choices and prices are confirmed when you enquire.

Can we talk to you about the wedding at the same time?

Yes. Weddings here can be anything from 4 to 100 guests, with evening receptions of up to 120, and the conservatory suits a smaller wedding party too. Ask and we’ll show you round while you’re here. There is more on our weddings page.

Can guests stay over?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite rooms in single, twin, double and family sizes. Ask about rooms when you book the party and we’ll hold them for your guests. Parking is free and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk.
