Why hold your meeting here

One room, one team, everything under one roof

Bentley Hotel is a Victorian house at the west end of Motherwell, between Glasgow and Edinburgh and a few minutes from the M74. Our function suite, the Back o’ Barns room, is air-conditioned and takes up to 120 people theatre-style or for a standing reception, and 100 seated for dinner. For a board meeting or a small training session, ask us about the conservatory or private dining in the restaurant.

Catering comes from our own kitchen, so tea and coffee on arrival, a working lunch or dinner are arranged with the same people who look after the room. Tell us about dietary needs and we’ll plan around them.

Getting people here is simple. Motherwell station, about 5 minutes’ walk, is on the West Coast Main Line, with Glasgow Central under 20 minutes away at the fastest, services to Edinburgh and direct trains from London and Manchester. Drivers leave the M74 at junction 6 and park on site for free; Glasgow Airport is about 30 minutes away and Edinburgh Airport about 45.

If your event runs into the evening, or delegates are travelling a long way, we have 18 en-suite bedrooms upstairs, each with a TV, tea and coffee making facilities and free WiFi, and a full Scottish breakfast cooked to order in the morning.

Room hire, catering and any equipment are quoted for each event, so tell us the date, your numbers and the layout you have in mind.

Questions

Meetings and conferences: what organisers ask us

How many people can the suite hold?

Up to 120 theatre-style or for a standing reception, and up to 100 seated for dinner. For other layouts, tell us what you have in mind and we’ll confirm the numbers.

Can you provide a projector, screen and WiFi?

WiFi is free throughout the hotel. Tell us what equipment your presenters need when you enquire and we’ll let you know what we can provide and what you may want to bring.

Is catering included?

Catering is from our own kitchen and arranged for each event, from tea and coffee to a seated dinner. We’ll quote it with room hire once we know your numbers and timings.

Can delegates stay overnight?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite bedrooms. Ask us about holding rooms for your delegates when you book the suite, and we’ll explain how they confirm their own bookings.

Where do delegates park, and how do they get here by train?

Parking on site is free. Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, with direct services to Glasgow, Edinburgh, London and Manchester. By road, leave the M74 at junction 6 (A723).
