Before you arrive

Booking and your stay

You can book online, see live availability or call us. Here’s how it works, and what to expect when you arrive.

How do I book a room?

The quickest way is online. Our booking page shows live availability and prices for every room, straight from our reservations diary, and confirms your booking there and then. Online booking runs on Cloudbeds, the system that manages the hotel’s reservations, so what you see is up to date. You can also call us on 01698 265588 and we’ll book your room over the phone.

Can I see which nights are free before I choose my dates?

Yes. Our availability calendar shows every room night by night, with a guide price, so you can spot a free night at a glance and then book those dates. If you can’t see what you need, call us on 01698 265588: for group bookings and longer stays we can often help.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2pm and check-out is by 11am. If you’d like to check in earlier or leave later, ask and we’ll help if we can on the day. If you arrive before 2pm or want to head out after 11am, ask us about leaving your bags with us in the meantime.

I’ll be arriving late in the evening. What should I do?

Please let us know your arrival time in advance, when you book or by phone, so we can make sure someone is here to welcome you and hand over your key. If your plans change on the day, call us on 01698 265588.

Is breakfast included in my room rate?

It depends on the rate you book. Your booking confirmation shows whether breakfast is included in your stay. If it isn’t, or you’re not sure, ask us when you book or call before you travel and we’ll tell you what we can arrange. Breakfast is a full Scottish, cooked to order. More about breakfast.

When do I pay, and what happens if I need to cancel?

Your booking confirmation sets out the payment and cancellation terms for the rate you chose, including when payment is taken and by when you can cancel. Terms differ between rates, so please read your confirmation. If anything in it is unclear, call us on 01698 265588 before your stay and we’ll talk it through.

Can I book several rooms for a group, a wedding or a work team?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite bedrooms: singles, twins, doubles and family rooms. For several rooms, call us on 01698 265588 or send an enquiry with your dates and rough numbers, and we’ll put the booking together for you and explain how each guest can confirm their room. Weekly stays for contractors work the same way.

Where you’ll sleep

Rooms

Eighteen en-suite bedrooms in a house built in 1874. The rooms page has photographs and a comparison table; the short answers are here.

What types of room do you have?

Four types, all en suite: a Single Room with one single bed for one adult, a Twin Room with two single beds for two, a Double Room with one double bed for two, and a Family Room with a double bed and a single bed, sleeping up to three guests, one of whom may be a child. Compare the rooms.

What is in every room?

Every room has an en-suite bathroom, a TV, tea and coffee making facilities and free WiFi. The whole hotel is non-smoking. Rooms vary in shape and size, as you’d expect in a house built in 1874, and some doubles have the bay windows that give the building its character.

Do you have family rooms, and can children stay?

Yes. Our family room has a double bed and a single bed and sleeps up to three guests, one of whom may be a child, so a couple with a child can share one room. Children are welcome in the restaurant too. If you’re travelling with a baby or need an extra bed, please call us on 01698 265588 before booking so we can tell you what we can offer.

Can I ask for a particular bed set-up or a quieter room?

Yes, and we’d encourage it. Rooms differ from one to the next, so if a particular bed arrangement matters to you, or you’d prefer a quieter room, tell us when you book or give us a call. We’ll do our best, and we’ll tell you honestly what is available for your dates.

Do the rooms have hairdryers, irons or a desk?

Ask us when you book and we’ll tell you what is in your room and what we can bring up for you. What we can promise in every room is an en-suite bathroom, a TV, tea and coffee making facilities and free WiFi.

Restaurant, conservatory & bar

Eating and drinking

Our kitchen cooks Scottish and British dishes for hotel guests and visitors alike. Opening times and menus change, so the restaurant page and a quick phone call are the best guides.

Does the hotel have a restaurant and bar?

Yes. Bentley Hotel has a locally renowned restaurant in the original house, a purpose-built conservatory alongside it and a bar. The kitchen cooks Scottish and British dishes and serves hotel guests and visitors alike. Restaurant & bar.

What is breakfast like?

A full Scottish breakfast, cooked to order when you come down, served in the restaurant or the conservatory. If you’d prefer something lighter, or you have a dietary requirement, tell us and we’ll see what the kitchen can do. We’ll confirm breakfast times when you check in. If you’re catching an early train, let us know the evening before and we’ll tell you what we can arrange.

When is the restaurant open, and do I need to book a table?

Opening times vary through the week and can change, so please call us on 01698 265588 before you set out, particularly on a Sunday or a bank holiday. We’d recommend booking a table, especially for dinner, Sundays and groups. Menus change too, so rather than print them we’ll tell you what’s on when you call.

Can I eat at the restaurant if I’m not staying at the hotel?

Yes. The restaurant, conservatory and bar are open to visitors as well as hotel guests. Book in the same way, by phone on 01698 265588, and park for free at the hotel while you eat.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary requirements?

Please tell us when you book and again when you order. Our kitchen will talk you through the dishes and the ingredients we use, and tell you plainly what we can and can’t do. Everything is prepared in the same kitchen, so if an allergy is severe please say so before you decide what to order. For a party or function, send us your guests’ requirements in advance. Dietary needs and allergies.

Can we book a table or a private space for a family occasion?

Yes. For a birthday, an anniversary, a christening lunch or a gathering after a funeral we can reserve a long table in the restaurant, the conservatory for a smaller party, or the function suite, which seats up to 100 for a meal. Tell us the occasion, the date and your numbers and we’ll suggest the right space and talk menus with you. Private dining and celebrations.

What is your food hygiene rating?

Our result under Scotland’s Food Hygiene Information Scheme is a Pass. The inspection was carried out by North Lanarkshire Council in December 2025, and you can check the result on the Food Standards Agency’s ratings site.

Celebrations & meetings

Weddings and events

Ceremony and reception under one roof, and a function suite for up to 120. There’s much more on our weddings and functions pages.

Can we hold our wedding ceremony and reception at Bentley Hotel?

Yes. Bentley Hotel is a venue for both the ceremony and the reception, for wedding parties of 4 to 100 guests and evening receptions of up to 120. The air-conditioned function suite is exclusively yours for a larger wedding, and the conservatory suits a smaller one. If you’d like to marry here, ask us about ceremonies when you enquire and we’ll explain what needs to be arranged. Weddings at Bentley Hotel.

What does the wedding package include?

An overnight stay in the hotel for the couple, fresh floral arrangements for all guest tables, personalised menus and a table plan, use of our wedding cake stand and knife, and a late licence until 1am. We offer an extensive choice of menus, or we can design the wedding around you. Send us a wedding enquiry for current details and available dates.

How many people can the function suite hold?

Up to 120, depending on the layout: 120 theatre-style or for a standing reception, and up to 100 for a seated dinner. The suite, known as the Back o’ Barns room, is air-conditioned, and our own kitchen does the catering. For smaller groups the conservatory may suit better. Functions & meetings.

What kinds of events and meetings do you host?

Meetings, conferences and training days, and birthdays, anniversaries, christenings and funeral receptions, as well as weddings. In-house catering ranges from tea and coffee to a full dinner. There is free parking at the hotel and Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, so it is easy for people to reach us.

How much does a wedding or function cost?

It depends on your numbers, the catering you choose and the hours you need, so we quote for each event rather than publishing prices. Send us your date, numbers and what you have in mind, or call 01698 265588, and we’ll put a written quote together for you.

Finding us

Getting here and parking

We’re at 19 High Road, Motherwell ML1 3HU, between Glasgow and Edinburgh, with the station a short walk away and the M74 close by. The location page has a map.

Where exactly is the hotel?

Bentley Hotel is at 19 High Road, Motherwell ML1 3HU, at the west end of the town, next door to the North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre and about 420 metres (around a 5-minute walk) from Motherwell railway station. Map and directions.

Is there parking?

Yes. There is free on-site parking at the hotel for guests, diners and people attending functions. There’s nothing to pay. If you’re coming in a minibus or with a trailer, let us know in advance.

How do I get here by train?

Motherwell station is on the West Coast Main Line, about 5 minutes’ walk from the hotel. ScotRail trains run frequently to Glasgow Central, the fastest taking under 20 minutes, and there are services to Edinburgh. Direct trains also run to London Euston and Manchester, and the Caledonian Sleeper stops at Motherwell. Check National Rail or ScotRail for current times.

How do I get here by car?

Motherwell sits right beside the M74. Leave at junction 6 (A723), signed for Hamilton, Motherwell and Wishaw. The M8 between Glasgow and Edinburgh is a few miles to the north. Glasgow is about 12 miles away and Edinburgh about 30. Parking at the hotel is free. Get directions.

Which airport is closest?

Glasgow Airport is roughly 20 miles away, around 30 minutes by car, and Edinburgh Airport roughly 30 miles, around 45 minutes, traffic permitting. Glasgow Prestwick is about 40 miles away. Call us if you’d like advice on the easiest way to reach us from your airport.

What is there to do nearby?

Next door is the North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre, which is free to visit and has a viewing tower. About a mile away are Strathclyde Country Park, with its loch and watersports, Fir Park, home of Motherwell FC, and the Dalzell Estate with the RSPB’s Baron’s Haugh reserve. M&D’s theme park and Amazonia are about 2 miles away, and Glasgow’s big venues are a direct train ride away. Things to do nearby.

House rules

Accessibility and policies

Bentley Hotel is a Victorian house, so we’d rather talk through access with you before you book than have you arrive to a surprise. Our other policies are short and simple.

Is there a lift, and do you have ground-floor rooms?

Bentley Hotel is a Victorian house and many of our bedrooms are reached by stairs. If stairs are a concern for you or anyone in your party, please call us on 01698 265588 before booking. We’ll tell you honestly what we can offer for your dates, including any ground-floor options, and talk you through the route into the building, the restaurant and the function suite. There’s more on our accessibility page.

Do you accept pets?

We’re sorry, but pets can’t stay at the hotel. Assistance animals are welcome. Let us know when you book so we can be ready for you.

Can I smoke at the hotel?

No. Bentley Hotel is a non-smoking hotel throughout: in the bedrooms, the bar and restaurant, the conservatory and the function suite.

Is there a minimum age to check in?

Yes. The guest checking in must be 18 or over. Children are welcome when they stay with an adult, and our family room sleeps up to three.
