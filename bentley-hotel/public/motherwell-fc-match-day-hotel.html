Match days

Fir Park, about a mile from our front door

Motherwell FC have played at Fir Park since 1895, and the ground is about a mile from Bentley Hotel: an easy walk, or a short taxi ride if the weather is doing what Lanarkshire weather does. Leave the car in our free car park, walk to the game and walk back without worrying about parking near the ground.

If you would like to eat before kick-off, call ahead. Our restaurant serves Scottish and British cooking, and opening times vary, so tell us which fixture you are coming for and we’ll let you know what we can do. After the final whistle there is a bar to come back to, a bed in the hotel and, if it is part of your rate, a full Scottish breakfast cooked to order in the morning.

For away days we are just as useful. Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, the fastest trains reach Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes, and Hampden Park and Celtic Park are both within about 15 miles, so you can stay with us, travel in by train and skip the city traffic.

Fixtures move for television and cup draws, so book as soon as your date is confirmed and check your confirmation for the cancellation terms that apply to your rate.

Questions

Match day questions

How far is Fir Park from the hotel?

About a mile, so it is an easy walk or a short taxi ride. Leave the car in our free car park rather than look for a space near the ground.

Can we eat at the hotel before the match?

Call ahead on 01698 265588. Opening times vary, so tell us which fixture you are coming for and we’ll let you know whether we can seat you before kick-off. The bar is here for afterwards.

Is there parking on match days?

Yes. Parking at the hotel is free for guests for the whole of their stay, on match days as on any other day.

We are following the team to Glasgow. How do we get there?

By train from Motherwell station, about 5 minutes’ walk from the hotel. The fastest trains reach Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes, and Hampden Park and Celtic Park are both within about 15 miles. Check the time of the last train back, or arrange a taxi.

Can we book several rooms for a group?

Yes. We have 18 en-suite rooms in all, so call us and we’ll talk through dates and room types. Twins and singles work well for groups of friends, and we’ll explain payment and cancellation terms when you book.
