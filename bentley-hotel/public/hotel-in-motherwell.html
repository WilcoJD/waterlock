Why stay with us

A hotel that makes Lanarkshire and both cities easy

Bentley Hotel sits on High Road at the west end of Motherwell, in a sandstone villa built in 1874 and known for many years as Motherwell House. It is part of the Hamilton Road Conservation Area, and from the outside it still looks like the family home it once was. Inside are 18 en-suite bedrooms, a restaurant with its own conservatory, a bar and a function suite.

What brings most people here is where we are. Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk, and the fastest trains reach Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes, with services to Edinburgh, direct trains to London and Manchester and the Caledonian Sleeper. By car, junction 6 of the M74 is close by and the M8 a few miles north; Glasgow is about 12 miles away and Edinburgh about 30.

So whether you are working in Lanarkshire, visiting family, here for a wedding or a match at Fir Park, or heading into Glasgow for the day, you can park with us for free, walk to the station and come back to a cooked meal.

Every room has an en-suite bathroom, a TV, tea and coffee making facilities and free WiFi. Because the house dates from 1874, no two rooms are quite the same shape, so if a particular bed set-up matters to you, tell us when you book.

Questions

Staying in Motherwell: what people ask us

Where exactly is Bentley Hotel?

At 19 High Road, Motherwell ML1 3HU, at the west end of the town. Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk and the North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre is next door. By road, leave the M74 at junction 6 (A723) and follow the signs for Motherwell.

Is parking free?

Yes. There is free parking on site for guests, for the whole of your stay.

Is breakfast included?

It depends on the rate you book, and your confirmation shows whether it is included. When it is, it’s a full Scottish breakfast cooked to order. Not sure? Call us on 01698 265588 and we’ll check.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2pm and check-out is by 11am. The main guest checking in must be 18 or over. If you expect to arrive late in the evening, let us know in advance so we can be ready for you.

How do I get into Glasgow or Edinburgh?

By train from Motherwell station, about 5 minutes’ walk away: Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes at the fastest, with services to Edinburgh too. By car, Glasgow is about 12 miles and Edinburgh about 30.
