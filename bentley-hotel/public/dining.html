The restaurant

Comfortable, spacious and welcoming

Our kitchen cooks Scottish and British dishes, served to hotel guests and visitors alike, in the restaurant or the conservatory beside it.

Whether you’re staying with us, meeting friends or gathering the family for a special occasion, you’ll find a warm welcome.

Please call us on 01698 265588 to hear about our current menus, check opening times and reserve a table.

Something for every occasion

From breakfast to family celebrations

Breakfast

A full Scottish breakfast, cooked to order. Hotel guests can check their booking confirmation to see whether breakfast is included in their stay.

Lunch & dinner

Honest Scottish and British cooking in the restaurant or the conservatory. Call ahead for today’s opening times.

Private dining

Birthdays, anniversaries, christenings and funeral receptions. We can reserve space for your group and talk menus with you.

The bar

A drink at the end of the day

The Bentley has its own bar alongside the restaurant: somewhere to unwind after a day’s work, raise a glass before dinner, or catch up after the match at Fir Park.

Bar and restaurant opening times can change, so please call us to check before you visit.
