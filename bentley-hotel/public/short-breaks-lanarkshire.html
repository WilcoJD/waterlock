Why base yourself here

A Victorian house with the whole central belt within reach

Motherwell is not the first place most people think of for a short break, which is part of its appeal. From our door you can be in Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes on the fastest trains, or in Edinburgh for the day, and come back in the evening to a street of Victorian villas, free parking and dinner under the same roof.

The house has a story of its own. It was built in 1874, so the hotel’s own history goes, for Colonel Black of the Cameronians, and was known for many years as Motherwell House, home to many of the town’s founding families. The sandstone exterior has kept its character, and the house now stands within the Hamilton Road Conservation Area, with the North Lanarkshire Heritage Centre next door.

Closer to hand, Strathclyde Country Park lies beside the Clyde about a mile away. Across the river in Hamilton are Chatelherault Country Park, built around William Adam’s hunting lodge of 1734, and the Hamilton Mausoleum; Bothwell Castle is a few miles away. To the south-east, the Clyde Valley follows the river upstream towards Lanark.

Every one of our 18 rooms is en suite, with a TV, tea and coffee making facilities and free WiFi; our doubles suit couples. Breakfast is a full Scottish, cooked to order, and the restaurant and bar are downstairs for the evening you don’t want to travel.

Questions

Planning a break: what people ask us

How do we get to Glasgow and Edinburgh without a car?

Motherwell station is about 5 minutes’ walk from the hotel. The fastest trains reach Glasgow Central in under 20 minutes, and there are services to Edinburgh too. Leave the car in our free car park.

Is there somewhere to eat at the hotel?

Yes. Our restaurant and conservatory serve Scottish and British cooking, and there is a bar. Opening times vary, so see the restaurant page or call ahead.

Is breakfast included?

That depends on the rate you book, and your confirmation shows whether it’s included. When it is, it’s a full Scottish breakfast cooked to order.

Is the hotel accessible?

Bentley Hotel is a Victorian house and many of our bedrooms are reached by stairs. If that is a concern, call us on 01698 265588 before booking and we’ll tell you honestly what we can offer. There is more on our accessibility page.

Can we arrive early or leave late?

We check guests in from 2pm and ask for rooms back by 11am. If you’re arriving earlier or leaving later, tell us when you book and we’ll let you know what we can arrange.
